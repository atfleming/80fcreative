**The Client**

Stax Records, one of the most iconic soul music labels, promoting its new high-definition “Memphis Masters” remasters.

**The Challenge**

Connect a timeless catalogue to a new generation by showing the living influence of Stax on today’s music.

**The Solution**

We created “The Memphis Masters,” interviewing contemporary artists about Stax’s influence. Shot in classic black-and-white to honor the legacy, the series bridged past and present with premium, historic aesthetic.

**The Results**

150k+ organic views and renewed engagement around the remasters — reframing Stax as a living, breathing influence, not just history.
