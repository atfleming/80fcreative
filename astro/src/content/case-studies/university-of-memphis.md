**The Client**

The University of Memphis is a cornerstone of education and culture in the region. In 2022, they were in the final stages of developing their new state-of-the-art music school, the Scheidt Performing Arts Center — a future hub for students, faculty, and the entire Memphis arts community.

**The Challenge**

With the landmark Scheidt Performing Arts Center nearing completion, the university's funding and donation drive had lost momentum. The UofM team needed a powerful tool to re-ignite their donor list and secure the final, critical contributions.

**The Solution**

80F Creative pivoted the creative direction from exposition to emotion with a cinematic, documentary-style film centered on faculty and students speaking directly to the viewer. We staged current students in the unfinished space and projected architectural plans onto the walls to connect people with the building’s future. A custom score amplified emotional resonance.

**The Results**

The film deeply resonated with donors. Distributed via newsletter, it re-ignited engagement and secured the necessary funding to complete the Scheidt Performing Arts Center — creating a lasting legacy for generations.
